Kurs

Driver app for taxi fleet drivers

Kurs helps drivers of our fleet with their daily work:

  • reserving a fleet vehicle for a shift,
  • recording trips and the kilometres driven,
  • sending photos of documents and fuel/expense receipts to the fleet office.

The app is used only by drivers who have been given access by the fleet. Photos are stored in the fleet operator's Google Drive.
